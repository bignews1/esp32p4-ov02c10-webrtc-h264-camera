Before publishing on GitHub, choose a license that matches your intention:

- MIT: simple permissive sharing
- Apache-2.0: permissive sharing with explicit patent language
- GPL-3.0: derivatives must remain open source
- No license: visible source, but no granted reuse rights

Also check the licenses of ESP-IDF, esp_h264, esp_peer, board support packages,
and any copied third-party code before publishing a complete repository.
